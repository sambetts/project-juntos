## Strategy critique

- **Problem and D-008.** [Banco de España, Annual Report 2025 ch. 2](https://www.bde.es/f/webbe/SES/Secciones/Publicaciones/PublicacionesAnuales/InformesAnuales/25/InfAnual_2025_En.pdf) supports affordability pain, not “most never try” or demand for shared debt. Compare renting, moving, waiting and buying with a known person. Start with **A: two households in one Madrid search area**, with plausible contributions and a common purchase window, not an age filter. B needs scarcer larger homes and adds safeguarding and scheduling; keep exploratory. C competes with established room offerings; a room-use right is not separate title. If comparing pages, hold channels/spend constant and compare qualified actions.
- **D-012 and gates.** 300 sign-ups → 120 profiles → 36 people saying “would consider” could yield zero viable pairs. Five of 15 saying they’d pay is not payment intent. “Fair test” and 100–299 are undefined. Fifteen half-hour interviews alone consume 7.5 of the 12 founder-hours in the €300 option. Pilot example, not benchmark: 20 eligible households, ≥6 two-way accepted introductions and ≥3 mutually approved briefs within 14 days; track cancellation, no-match and second meetings. Zero acceptance after adequate eligible exposure warrants rethinking; under-recruitment is inconclusive. Before Phase 2 require written legal/perimeter review, a credible lender route for the borrower count and safety controls. Three viewings and an offer do not justify an MVP; prove a safe close first or stay manual. Scale after repeatable closes and jurisdiction checks.
- **D-010.** Test matching value before pricing legal services. S.L. shareholders own shares, not the home; compare tax, administration, occupancy, lending and liquidity. Separate units require planning and registry feasibility. Check title, charges, permitted use, community rules and financing before arras; protect against one borrower dropping out.
- **Risks.** Raise R-001 and R-008 to 3×3 for planning, R-006 to 3×3 for release as built, R-003 and R-011 to 2×3 until classification and permissions are controlled, and R-009 to 2×2 until clearance. Split R-002 into approval risk and agreed solidarity exposure. Score R-004 over ownership, not six weeks; R-005 is deferred, not solved. ## Prototype critique

- **Run:** `cd prototype && node test_proto.js` exited 0. Family: five eligible; Laura 92%, Marta/Javi 91%; adding Laura after Marta/Javi was refused. Room: 12 eligible; group minima 97/88/81% for 2/3/4 households. `group=best` picks the last displayed size, not the highest score. The harness prints rather than asserts outcomes; no actual browser was tested.
- **Logic:** Bilateral checks and whole-group intersections work: three pairs with area overlap but no three-way overlap were correctly rejected. Six bedrooms is a demo cap, not human incompatibility. Uncalibrated weights: 40/20/20/10/10. A narrow preference inside a broad one gets full place points; same household adds two points. No wish-lists scores better than one unique step-free requirement (five points); `must` and `nice` score identically for the same feature. Keep access needs as property requirements; hide sensitive bios and private rejection reasons, and consider assistance animals separately.
- **State and sizing:** Nuria is 70 vs a 75 threshold yet can be added twice through the handler. Preview overwrites the saved group without acceptance. Changing the room preset to four bedrooms kept an invalid seven-bedroom group and still drew the brief. Require opt-in, deduplication, revalidation and joint brief approval. Formula matches the components plus 15%, rounded to nearest 5 m². Family + Marta/Javi shows 160 m² for eight people, two private baths and an uncounted suggested WC. Use an editable range, distinguish *útil* from *construida*, and test real layouts. Three rounded 33% room allocations are neither area nor equity.
- **Accessibility:** White on `#d9480f` is 4.30:1; orange on `#fff0e8` is 3.87:1, both below [WCAG AA](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/contrast-minimum). Replace light `--brand:#d9480f` with `--brand:#b3370e` for those pairs. Tabs lack [associated panels and arrow keys](https://www.w3.org/WAI/ARIA/apg/patterns/tabs/); rerendering can drop focus. Test focus, labels, zoom, mobile, dark mode and storage/reset in browsers. Use Spanish and `lang="es"` for user testing. Replace “Liked, waiting for them” with explicitly simulated copy; never imply a real reply or calibrated odds.
