# Market and competitors (Spain and Europe), 2026-11-18, corrected 2026-11-20

**Status:** superseded on 2026-11-20 by `research/2026-11-20-competitors.md` (B-001), which maps each rival's actual flow with sources. Kept for history.

## Summary
- Rivals overlap more than our first scan said. habitacion.com and Casicasa offer affinity-based matching and group home search; Yorsio offers affinity matching and rent-to-own.
- Vivla and Pacaso are holiday-home co-ownership models: useful comparisons, not direct rivals. A Pacaso page for Spain doesn't prove current Spanish inventory.
- "Nobody covers matching through to a home and an exit" is unproven. Our possible distinction: strangers form a group *before* choosing a whole home, approve a joint brief together and plan the exit from day one.
- Market signal (*Reported*): elEconomista (March 2026), drawing on the mortgage broker iAhorro, wrote that buying a home with several strangers is no longer seen as crazy.
