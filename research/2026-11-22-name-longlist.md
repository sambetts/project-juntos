# Name longlist, 2026-11-22 (B-026)

(Brand & Marketing.) Sam asked for name suggestions. This adds new candidates to the shortlist in `research/2026-11-20-naming-screen.md`, where Compicasa is still the provisional favourite (D-007). It's a quick public screen, not clearance: no trademark register was searched this time (B-024 covers that), and nothing was bought, filed or sent.

## Summary
- **Recommendation:** put **Sumallaves** next to Compicasa as the second favourite, and take both into the clearance search (B-024) and the Spanish-speaker comprehension test. Sumallaves is a made-up word, so it should be easier to protect than an everyday phrase, and it says "pooling" without saying "flat-share".
- Of 71 names checked, about 20 had neither the .com nor the .es registered (method below). Most of them are descriptive phrases, which are weaker as trademarks.
- **Condueños** is the clearest meaning of any candidate ("co-owners"), but because it's an ordinary word it may be hard to register as a mark (Assumption; for the lawyer or the clearance search).
- Names that only work for two people (Casa de Dos, Dos Dueños, Casa al Alimón) fit pairs-only pilots but not groups (D-002), so they rank lower.
- **Found in passing:** Viviendea, a platform that "groups people looking for housing according to their interests" and then approaches a developer (Reported: construible.es, 2018-07-09). It isn't in our rival review (B-001). I've proposed it for the next competitor watch (B-019).

## Method
- **.com (Verified, 2026-11-22):** the registry's public lookup (Verisign RDAP, `rdap.verisign.com/com/v1/domain/<name>.com`). "Not found" means not registered. Prices weren't checked; .com was listed at €21.99 on 2026-11-20.
- **.es (Assumption, indicative only):** public DNS (Google, 8.8.8.8) showed no name servers. An .es address can be registered without name servers, so confirm it on dominios.es or a registrar before deciding. .es was listed at €10.99 on 2026-11-20.
- **Existing use (Reported):** one web search per top name, with no portal pages opened. Trademark registers (OEPM, EUIPO, TMview) weren't searched this time.
- Accents and the ñ are dropped from web addresses (condueno, alalimon).
