# Name screening, 2026-11-20

**Status:** provisional screening, corrected after the independent review. It isn't legal clearance: availability changes daily, and the trademark search only finds marks that literally contain the words, not similar or sound-alike ones. The screenshots weren't archived, so re-run the checks and keep the results (date, query, territory, class, status) before deciding.

## Method
- **Web addresses:** GoDaddy bulk search (Spain site), 33 names × .es and .com = 66 addresses. 28 were free to register; 7 names had both free. Listed prices: .es €10.99, .com €21.99.
- **Trademarks:** TMView "contains" search across the Spanish (OEPM), EU (EUIPO) and WIPO registers. "No rows found" was confirmed on screen.
- **Businesses:** a web search per shortlisted name.

Judgement calls: Compicasa is warm, but "compi" can suggest casual flat-sharing, so pair it with a clear descriptor (the review suggested "Encuentra con quién comprar tu vivienda"). Mismo Techo is clearer but commonplace, so harder to protect. Casacita and La Otra Mitad sound romantic. Halfnest needs explaining and implies halves. Arrejuntaos is memorable but cheeky and suggests a couple. Test comprehension and trust with Spanish speakers before choosing.

## To lock a name in (Sam's actions, after F-001)
1. A professional clearance search: exact, fuzzy and phonetic variants and prior use, for the services we'll actually offer.
2. Sam approves the name (D-007).
3. Buy the .es, and the .com if free (€32.98 for both, listed on 20 Nov).
4. File a trademark at the OEPM (or EUIPO for the EU), choosing the goods and services terms for what we'll actually do, rather than classes 36, 45 and 42 automatically.
5. The company-name certificate from the Registro Mercantil Central is separate from the trademark and domain checks; get it when incorporating.
