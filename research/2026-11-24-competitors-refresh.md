# Competitors refresh: Spain and abroad (2026-11-24)
**Status:** Research, Session 27, for `project/strategy.md` (D-048). Updates `research/2026-11-20-competitors.md` (B-001), which keeps the detailed flows. Labels: Verified (company's own page opened on 2026-11-24) / Reported / Assumption. No portal pages opened.

## Summary
- **Still nobody in Spain matches strangers into a group that buys one whole home together** with a joint brief, voting and exit rules (searched; no new entrant found). Casicasa is closest in concept but now says plainly it has "no search for people and no process to request joining a group" (Verified).
- **habitacion.com is leaning towards investors:** its homepage leads with rooms "desde 35.000€", studios from €90,000 and whole rental flats from €150,000 (Verified). 2024: 80+ sales, €1.4M revenue, not yet profitable (Reported).
- **Abroad, matching strangers for a main home has no surviving success story.** Kohab (Australia, 2018, with a bespoke NAB mortgage) now redirects to a "domain for sale" page (Verified); Husmates (Toronto) matched two groups of strangers, one of which bought, and now leads with buying "with a Friend" (Reported / Verified).
- **The lasting business is the agreement, not the match.** CoBuy (US, 2016) never matched strangers, started free to buyers on agents' commission, and in 2023 switched to software for co-owners: $1,000 a year per group of 2–4, against $7,500–15,000+ for a lawyer-drafted agreement (Verified, its own pages).
- **Free mortgage brokers (iAhorro "totalmente gratuito", Verified) don't handle groups:** partners, not rivals.

## Where we are different, and where we can do better
| Point | Us (Compicasa, as built) | Best rival |
|---|---|---|
| Group forms before any home exists, from open matching | Yes: likes, mutual matches, groups of 2–4 with invitations (D-002, D-039) | Casicasa: private groups only; Husmates: yes, tiny |
| A whole home the group chooses, with a joint brief | Yes, brief from members' profiles; homes filtered by the brief and combined budgets | habitacion.com: a room in a home it chose |
| Group decisions on homes | Shortlist with three hidden-vote rounds (D-043) | CoBuy: governance after purchase |
| Cost per person, with each region's tax | Yes: every region's ITP scale Verified (B-060) | Casicasa: an indicative share, taxes excluded |
| Real homes | Agencies' own adverts, linked (D-035), across Spain (D-045) | Casicasa: portal previews |
| Live in or rent out | Both, never mixed (D-047) | habitacion.com: rooms, studios and investor flats |
| Free, with no margin in the price | Yes (D-048) | Casicasa free; operators build margins in |
| Spanish and English | Yes (D-024) | Mostly Spanish only (Assumption) |
| **Gaps where rivals are ahead** | No exit or agreement tool yet; no lending route; no completed purchase; unknown brand; no human support | habitacion.com buy-back and notarised pact; CoBuy agreements |
