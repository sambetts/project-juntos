## Registrability (for the lawyer)
- Spanish law refuses marks that lack distinctive character, or that consist **exclusively** of words describing the service (Ley 17/2001, arts. 5.1.b and 5.1.c; Verified, BOE, 2026-11-22). Compicasa blends a slang word with "casa", so it's arguably distinctive, but that's the lawyer's call. The same rule is why Condueños looked weak in the longlist.
- Earlier marks block a new one when the marks are identical or similar, the services are identical or similar, and the public might confuse them (art. 6.1.b). This counts Spanish marks, international marks covering Spain, and EU marks (art. 6.2.a; Verified).

## Steps for the register search (about ten minutes, in a browser)
1. **TMView** (https://www.tmdn.org/tmview/): offices ES, EM and WO; search each of COMPICASA, COMPRICASA, COMPRACASA, COMPRARCASA and COMPI (as "contains"); note any live marks in classes 35, 36, 42 or 45.
2. **OEPM Localizador** (https://consultas2.oepm.es/LocalizadorWeb/): the same terms, "contenga".
3. Record for each result: the date, query, office, mark, owner, classes and status. A screenshot or the exported list goes in this file, as text, with no personal data about private owners.
4. Optional: confirm compicasa.es is free on dominios.es.
