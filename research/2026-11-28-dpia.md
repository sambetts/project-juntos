# DPIA, data map and record of processing for the free app, 2026-11-28 (B-027, B-052)

(Legal & Compliance.) For the free public release (D-048, release checklist item 5 in `project/strategy.md`). It replaces section 2 of `research/2026-11-22-privacy.md` for the app; that note's data map was for the hand-run pilot. No lawyer has reviewed it (D-050): it records our own reasoning, not legal advice. It holds no personal data, so it lives in the repository. Facts about the app come from its code as of Session 39 (Verified in code, file named); legal points carry their label.

## Summary
- **A DPIA is needed and this is it.** The app meets at least two of the AEPD's art. 35.4 criteria: profiling of habits (1) and data revealing financial situation (4), now that profiles hold a maximum budget figure (D-044). Reported: criteria checked on the AEPD's list on 2026-11-22 (B-013).
- **Residual risk is acceptable for a small, free release** with the measures in section 2. The main risks left are scams or harassment between members, with one person (Sam) reviewing reports, and self-declared age. No prior consultation with the AEPD (art. 36) looks needed, since no risk stays high (Assumption).
- **Everything a person gives us goes when they delete their account**, including their External ID sign-in record, except reports others made about them (kept up to two years for safety) and backups (7 days). Built in Session 39 (B-039).
- **Moderation is by a person, with reasons** (B-041): reports reviewed by an admin, suspensions with a statement of reasons and an appeal through the contact form. To review a report, an admin can read the last 20 messages of the reported conversation, and the privacy notice says so.
- **Still open:** the operator's identity in the notice (F-037); running `app/infra/erase-signins.ps1` so sign-in records are removed (needs Sam's sign-in); a target time to answer reports (Sam).

## 1. Data map (the app as built)
Everything is stored in Azure SQL `sqldb-compicasa` (UK South) unless the row says otherwise. "Delete" means the account deletion in `Api.cs` (`DELETE /api/me`), which runs as one transaction.

**Not collected:** date of birth, photos of people, ID documents, nationality, income or savings figures beyond the budget, religion, health, exact address. Household type never filters a real person (B-042). Signed-out visitors get no cookies (Verified on the live site, 2026-11-28; App Service affinity cookies are off in `resources.bicep`). The browser keeps the language and colour scheme in local storage.

## 2. DPIA
**Necessity.** Each item in section 1 serves something the person asked for or a safety duty. Choices that keep data down: a nickname is fine; no photos; areas, not addresses; the budget never touches matching; dealbreakers and habits stay private; feedback keeps the kind of screen, never ids (D-052); funnel counts aren't stored.

**Automated decisions (art. 22).** Matching suggests and ranks people, and people decide whom to like and whom to group with. Moderation is decided by a person. No decision with legal or similarly significant effect is automated (Assumption that ranking suggestions isn't such a decision).

| Risk to people | Likelihood / harm | Measures | Left |
|---|---|---|---|
| A breach exposes habits, budgets or messages | Low / high | Entra-only database sign-in with the app's managed identity, no passwords; TLS; strict CSP and CSRF header; the admin list is set in the web app's settings | Low |
| A member scams, pressures or harasses another (money for a "deposit") | Medium / high | Code of conduct before the first message ("never send money, bank details or ID documents"); report and block on every profile and conversation; human review; suspension with reasons; the reporter is told | **Medium**: no proactive monitoring, one reviewer |
| Budgets or habits inferred by other members | Medium / low | Only a group's total; the notice warns about pairs; habits never shown | Low |
| Discrimination, including by proxy | Low / high | No protected traits asked or used (D-009); household type never filters real people (B-042); money filters homes only | Low |
| Someone is found or followed offline | Low / high | No photos, surnames or addresses; areas only; block hides both sides | Low |
| Data kept longer than needed | Low / medium | All-or-nothing deletion; External ID record removed; reports purged after 2 years; contact messages after 1; backups roll off in 7 days (Assumption: Basic tier's retention, not checked) | Low, once the removal script has run |
| An admin reads private messages | Low / medium | Only a reported conversation, only its last 20 messages; admins listed by account id; the notice says so | Low |
| A borrowed or stolen session deletes or downloads an account | Low / medium | Deleting needs a sign-in within the last 10 minutes, with External ID asked to prompt for the password; a typed confirmation word. The download needs a signed-in session only | Low (download: Medium, accepted) |
| A minor signs up | Medium / medium | 18+ box at sign-up; terms say 18+ | **Medium**: self-declared only, accepted for a free tool |
| Hosting on a development subscription (D-049) | Low / medium | Microsoft is still the processor under its standard data protection terms (Assumption: the terms cover this subscription type); a move is ready (B-073) | Low for people; contractual for us (R-024) |

**Conclusion.** With these measures, no risk to people stays high. Review this DPIA before charging, at 1,000 finished profiles, after any serious incident, or if a new kind of data is added. An AI drafted it; Sam signs it off as controller (no lawyer, D-050).
