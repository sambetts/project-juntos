# Glossary: Spanish property terms

Plain-English orientation only, not legal advice. Legal & Compliance verifies before anything relies on it.

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Comunidad de bienes / pro indiviso | Co-ownership: each owner holds an undivided percentage share (cuota) of the whole property (Código Civil arts. 392–406) |
| Cuota | Each co-owner's share, for example 50% |
| Acción de división | Any co-owner's right to ask to end the co-ownership (art. 400 CC), subject to any no-division pact. If the home is essentially indivisible and the owners don't agree to award it to one of them, who compensates the others, it's sold and the money shared (art. 404 CC). Lenders' and other third parties' rights survive (art. 405) |
| Pacto de indivisión | Agreement to keep the property undivided for up to 10 years, renewable by a new agreement (art. 400 CC) |
| Retracto de comuneros | When a co-owner sells their share to an outsider, the other co-owners can step into the buyer's place on the same terms, within nine days of registration or, failing that, of learning of the sale (arts. 1522 and 1524 CC). It's not a veto |
| Extinción de condominio | Ending the co-ownership, typically by awarding the property to one owner who pays the others. For an indivisible home awarded to one owner with fair compensation, it's usually taxed as AJD rather than ITP; income tax (IRPF) can still apply to a gain |
| Escritura pública / notario | The purchase deed, signed before a notary |
| Registro de la Propiedad / nota simple | The Land Registry / an extract showing owners and charges |
| Arras | Deposit contract signed before completion |
| ITP | Transfer tax on resale homes; each region sets the rate |
| IVA + AJD | VAT plus stamp duty on new-build homes |
| Plusvalía municipal (IIVTNU) | Municipal tax on the rise in urban land value when a property changes hands |
| IRPF, ganancia patrimonial | Income tax on the gain when you sell, including selling a share |
| Responsabilidad solidaria | Joint and several liability: each borrower can be pursued for the whole debt |
| Hoja de visita | Viewing sheet signed at an agency. A signature alone doesn't bind the buyer to a fee: what matters is a clear prior agreement, the services given, and unfair-terms rules |
| API | Estate agent (Agente de la Propiedad Inmobiliaria). Catalonia and Valencia have mandatory agent registers; Madrid's is voluntary |
| S.L. | Private limited company |
| CNMC | Spain's competition authority |
| AEPD | Spain's data protection authority |
| OEPM / EUIPO | Spanish and EU trademark offices |
| Registro Mercantil Central | Central companies register; issues the company-name certificate needed to incorporate |
| Hoja de encargo | A lawyer's engagement letter: the scope, the fee and what's excluded |
| Colegiado | A registered, practising member of a professional body, such as a bar association |
| EIPD | Evaluación de impacto relativa a la protección de datos: the Spanish name for a DPIA |
| Carta de intenciones | A letter of intent; it can be written so that it binds no one |
