# Charter (v0.2, 2026-11-20, after the independent review)

_Since 2026-11-24 (D-048), `project/strategy.md` sets the strategy: a free public release instead of the hand-run pilot, with fresh evidence on the problem and competitors. Where the two differ, the strategy wins._

## Problem
Housing is hard to afford in Spain: the Banco de España's 2025 annual report (chapter 2) documents the strain. It doesn't show demand for buying with strangers rather than renting, moving, waiting or buying with someone you know. That's the open question (B-004 and the pilot). Buying with others is legally possible, but finding the right people, agreeing what to buy, getting a mortgage and planning a safe exit are hard.

## Idea
Match compatible people, dating-style, to buy one home together. Turn their profiles into a joint brief ("what our home needs"). Help them find the home. Later, handle the legal set-up and exits for a fee.

## Who it's for (D-008)
- **First (proposed):** two households searching in one Madrid area, with similar budgets and a shared purchase window.
- Exploratory: families pooling with another household for a bigger home.
- Later: people who only want a room's share. Room offers already exist, and a right to use a room isn't separate title.

## Why us (hypothesis, unproven)
B-001 (2026-11-20, `research/2026-11-20-competitors.md`) found that, on public evidence, no rival lets strangers form a group before choosing a whole home, approve a joint brief together and design their own exit.

But much of the rest already exists:
- affinity questionnaires: habitacion.com's h.armony, Yorsio's cohabitation profile and Casicasa's MAT
- co-ownership shares (pro indiviso) with a notarised owners' pact: habitacion.com
- exits: habitacion.com's buy-back, and Vivla's and Pacaso's resale services

habitacion.com sells a share with the use of one room, choosing the home first. It reports 250 rooms and studios sold and has raised €1.7M.

Our possible distinction: the group comes first and buys a whole home; mixed households with unequal shares; a joint brief; and exit rules the group agrees before it chooses a home. The pilot must show that strangers will commit to a group before seeing a home.

## Scope for now (Phases 0–1)
- **In:** matching, the joint brief, validation with real people, research, partner conversations.
- **Out:** handling money, charging anyone, arranging mortgages, giving legal advice, showing portal listings.

## Hypotheses to test
- H1: Enough people in Madrid want to co-buy with someone they haven't met yet.
- H2: They'll complete a detailed profile and act on their matches.
- H3: Groups formed this way stay together until they buy.
- H4: Agencies will pay for, or waive buyer fees for, ready-to-buy groups.
- H5: A legal structure makes exits safe enough for strangers.
- H6: Banks or brokers will finance these groups on acceptable terms.

## Proposed pilot measures (Sam to approve: F-005)
These are examples, not benchmarks.
- Recruit about 20 eligible households in one Madrid search area (only after every gate in section 2 of the pilot plan and Sam's dated go-ahead).
- Success within 14 days: at least 6 introductions accepted by both sides, and at least 3 joint briefs approved by every member.
- Group-first commitment (M3): at least 2 pairs complete the exit-rules worksheet and sign a non-binding pledge, with no money involved. Full measures and decision rules: section 8 of `product/phase1-validation-plan.md`.
- Also track no-matches, cancellations and second meetings, always with the denominators.
- Rethink if there are no two-way acceptances after enough eligible people have seen matches. Too few sign-ups is inconclusive, not a "no".
- Interviews are for learning, not a success measure: saying you'd pay isn't paying.
- Before Phase 2: a lawyer's written review of the structure and of our role, a credible lending route for the number of borrowers, and safety controls.

## Constraints
Spain and Spanish law; a founder with a full-time job and an employer check pending (F-001); a small budget (F-002); no scraping or portal listings; nothing public, and no real users, without Sam.
