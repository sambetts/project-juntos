The CEO refines this; Sam approves each gate with a dated go-ahead.

| Phase | Goal | Main work | Gate to the next phase |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 Explore and set up (now) | Shape the idea and the AI team | Research, prototype, naming, the project files, the independent review | Every gate in section 2 of `product/phase1-validation-plan.md` (employer clearance, workspace, time and budget, the pilot's design and measures, the lawyer's Part A answers, the privacy and safety groundwork, the Spanish materials and interview guide), then Sam's dated go-ahead |

| 3 Product MVP | Self-service matching | Accounts and verification, matching, a group space, an agreement workflow, licensed listing data | Repeatable completed purchases; unit economics understood |
| 4 Grow | More cities and services | Finance partners, a marketplace for reselling shares, more cities | Legal and regulatory checks for each new region |
