## In short
**Strategy since 2026-11-24 (D-048, `project/strategy.md`):** a free public release instead of the hand-run pilot; charge only at critical mass, and matching stays free for good. Nothing opens until the release checklist (strategy section 4) is met and Sam approves it as the gate (F-033). The idea: help compatible strangers buy one home together in Spain, starting with the social side (matching plus a joint "what our home needs" brief). On public evidence, no rival lets strangers form a group before choosing a whole home, but affinity questionnaires, co-ownership shares with a notarised pact, and exit services already exist, so the group-first idea is what we must test. A working proof of concept now runs in Azure, in UK South on Sam's existing App Service plan with a free database (closed, made-up people, no extra cost: D-036); nothing else is public or bought. The project is the private GitHub repository project-juntos (D-022), and is also a proof of concept for building a new company with AI (D-026). Sam's plan (2026-11-21): build a small product and see if it gains interest. The product is Spanish first, with English supported; project documents stay in English (D-024). The pilot can't run until every gate in section 2 of its plan is met.
