## In short
The idea: help compatible strangers buy one home together in Spain, starting with the social side (matching plus a joint "what our home needs" brief). On public evidence, no rival lets strangers form a group before choosing a whole home, but affinity questionnaires, co-ownership shares with a notarised pact, and exit services already exist, so the group-first idea is what we must test. Nothing is public, nothing has been bought and no money has been spent. For now it's a thought experiment.
